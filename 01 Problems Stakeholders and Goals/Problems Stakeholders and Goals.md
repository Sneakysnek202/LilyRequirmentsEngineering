# Problems Stakeholders and Goals
## The Current Situation
A college provides a range of student support services, such as academic support, careers advice, learning support and other student services.
At present, students may contact services by email or phone, while staff use calendars, spreadsheets and other informal methods to manage appointments.
The college wants to investigate a software system that could make appointment management easier (Sourced from the Project Brief)

## The Main Problem
The main problem is that there is a lot of confusion just all around. Students are confused as to which service they should contact, the current systems in place may not be accessible. Staff may get confused with managing appointments manually, and managing cancellations and changes. There could also be confusion with staff availability and appointment types.

## Who is Affected
1. Students 
2. Support Staff
3. IT Staff

## Important Stakeholders
1. College
2. Students
3. Support Staff
4. IT Staff

## Stakeholder Needs and Concerns
1. College - They need an organised way for students and staff to manage appointment.
2. Students - They need a formal way to book any and all support appointments, that is easy to understand and easy to find and shows staff availability and felxibility.
3. Support Staff - They need a formal way to manage appointment bookings, schedules, and their availability.
4. IT Staff - They need to understand how the proposed solution works and how to fix it should it go down.

## Goals of Proposed Situation
1. Accessible and easy to use for any and all students and staff.
2. Provides a quick clear way to book appointments, and clear distinction between each support service
3. Provides a a simple yet formal and secure way for staff to manage bookings and vulnerable student information.
4. Good security for vulnerable information.
5. Cost effective
6. Easy to manage, maintain and fix should something go wrong.
